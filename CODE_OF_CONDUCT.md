# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Attack claims, never people.

## Specifics that matter in a repository built on measurement

* **Disagree with evidence.** "This is wrong" is welcome when followed by a
  command and its output. A benchmark, a failing test, a counterexample and a
  diff are all arguments; volume is not.
* **A correction is a contribution.** Finding a false green here is the most
  valuable thing you can do, and it will be credited as such — several of the
  defects recorded in `NOTICE.md` were found by the author against their own
  work and written down rather than quietly fixed.
* **No harassment, no personal attacks, no dogpiling.** Nothing about someone's
  identity, background, or level of experience belongs in a technical thread.
* **Assume the other person read less than you think and more than you fear.**
  Ask before concluding they are careless.

## Enforcement

Report to the repository maintainers via a private advisory or a direct issue.
Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a request to edit a
comment through to a permanent ban, proportionate to the conduct.

This document is deliberately short. A longer one would not make anyone kinder.
