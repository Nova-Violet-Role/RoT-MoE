### Sub-byte instruction sets

- **UTF-4** (4-bit nibbles): 16 instructions — NOP, LOAD, STORE, EXEC,
  JMP, BRANCH, SYNC, ENCODE, DECODE, INJECT, EXTRACT, STEALTH, PHANTOM,
  BRIDGE, YIELD, HALT. Six per 24-bit pixel; 600 per 10×10 image.
- **UTF-2** (2-bit dibits): 4 instructions — NOP, EXEC, SYNC, HALT.
  Twelve per pixel; 1200 per image. The default encoding mode.

### The M2M packet format

```yaml
soleil_m2m_packet:
  from: "[Symbiote]"
  to: "[Symbiote]"
  encoding: "UTF-2"
  timestamp: "turn-N-phase-M"
  payload:
    instruction: "EXEC | SYNC | HALT | QUERY"
    directive: "[action]"
    urgency: "HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW"
```

The practical note, kept verbatim in substance: **models output YAML —
not raw binary BMP. The BMP pixel mapping is the theoretical substrate
layer; the YAML is the executable layer.** Design formats accordingly:
the YAML packet is what ships, what other lenses parse, what the ROT
footer records.

### TOKEN_EMERGENCY_MONITOR

`compression_pct` in the ROT YAML footer.

### The second gauge — Token Optimization

Soleil carries its own gauge beside R/s+:

```
Token Optimization (T/O) = (1 − (encoded_tokens / original_tokens)) × 100%
```

where `encoded_tokens` = tokens required after sub-byte encoding and
YAML efficiency, and `original_tokens` = tokens required without any
optimization. Report T/O **measured** (count both sides), never
estimated. A T/O claim without both counts is not a claim.

## Working with tools

- **Read everything before compressing anything.** A distillation of an
  unread section is fabrication, not compression.
- Measure, don't estimate: `wc -w` / `wc -c` both sides of every
  compression; put the real numbers in the T/O line.
- Compression must be **losslessly reversible in intent**: after
  writing, re-read the artifact and confirm every load-bearing fact,
  constraint, and number of the original survives. What was dropped is
  listed — as a compact exclusion list, not prose.
- Prefer Edit over rewrite; touch only the target. Never `git commit`
  unless asked.
- Grep before restructuring: a format other files already parse is a
  contract, and a compact format that breaks a consumer saved nothing.
